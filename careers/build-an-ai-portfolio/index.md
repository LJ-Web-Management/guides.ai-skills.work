# Make your learning visible

Build an honest AI skills portfolio with useful projects, clear methods, and verifiable results.

Source: https://lj-web-management.github.io/guides.ai-skills.work/careers/build-an-ai-portfolio/
Updated: 2026-10-28
Publisher: ai-skills.work

## Choose projects with a real purpose

Select a small problem relevant to the work you want to do. A source-backed briefing, an accessible presentation, or a tested spreadsheet workflow can be more informative than a flashy demonstration with no clear purpose. Use public, licensed, or synthetic inputs and protect information from your employer or clients.

- State who the project helps and why.
- Define what success would look like before starting.

## Document your process and decisions

Explain the inputs, tools, instructions, review steps, and changes you made. Include an example of a weak result and how you corrected it. Make your own contribution explicit. If you report a time saving or quality improvement, explain how you measured it and the limits of that comparison.

- Keep prompts and source material where sharing is permitted.
- Record errors as well as improvements.

## Present a clear, honest case study

Use a short structure: problem, method, result, and lessons. Provide a working example or screenshots where appropriate. Avoid invented clients, qualifications, or performance claims. A useful portfolio helps someone understand how you think, not just that a tool produced an attractive output.

- Explain what you would change next.
- Check accessibility, permissions, and links before sharing.

## In practice: A portfolio outline

Problem: a public guide is difficult to navigate. Method: use AI to propose a structure, verify content manually, and test navigation with a colleague. Result: show the revised guide and describe feedback without claiming unmeasured business impact.

## Try it yourself

Draft a one-page case study outline for a project you can complete using public or fictional information.

## Common questions

### Can I include workplace projects?

Only with permission and appropriate removal of confidential information. A recreated example using synthetic inputs may be safer.

### Should I mention AI assistance?

Describe the tools and your contribution clearly. Follow any relevant employer, educational, or publishing disclosure rules.
