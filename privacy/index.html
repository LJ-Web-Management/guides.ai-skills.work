A little clarity about privacy

How this learning center handles searches, quiz answers, and browser requests.

No account needed

This learning center does not ask you to create an account, provide an email address, or submit personal information to read guides or take quizzes.

Search and quizzes

Search loads a static guide index and filters it in your browser. The search phrase may appear in your browser address bar, history, and hosting request logs when a search URL is opened. Quiz answers are held in the current page’s memory, used to calculate a result locally, and reset when the page reloads. This application does not send your selections or search terms to an AI service.

Cookies and analytics

The application does not set analytics or advertising cookies and does not include third-party analytics scripts. Fonts and application assets are served with the site. Hosting infrastructure may process standard request information, such as an IP address, requested URL, and user agent, to operate and secure the service.

External destinations

Links to the main ai-skills.work website and external sources lead to separate services with their own policies. This learning center’s behavior does not establish how those services handle information.

Browser agent tools

In compatible browsers, WebMCP tools can search or read public guide content and list quizzes. They do not read quiz answers, submit attempts, access accounts, or send messages. Your browser or agent provider may have its own data practices.

Questions

Use the current official contact options on ai-skills.work for privacy questions. Do not include credentials or confidential records in a message.
