# Choose your next small step

Use the learning paths and lesson exercises to build practical AI knowledge at your own pace.

## Start with your immediate goal

Choose foundations if the concepts feel unfamiliar, practical skills if you have a task in mind, or career guidance if you are deciding what to learn next. The homepage highlights a short beginner route. These paths are suggestions, not entry requirements or qualifications.

- Read the summary before opening a longer guide.
- Skip familiar material and return when you need it.

## Practice before moving on

Each guide includes a worked example and a small exercise. Use public or invented information and check the result yourself. If an exercise feels difficult, return to the relevant section or try a simpler example. Understanding one task well is more useful than rushing through several pages.

- Write down one thing you learned.
- Keep a note of a question that still needs an answer.

## Check understanding and revisit gaps

Quizzes provide feedback on selected concepts. They are learning tools, not certifications or employment assessments. Review the explanations, return to the linked lessons, and try a new example. Your browser bookmarks can help you keep track of pages you want to revisit.

- Retake a quiz after reviewing its explanations.
- Use real-world practice to complement quiz results.

## In practice: A practical route

For a workplace goal, read the privacy guide, learn the prompt structure, and choose a task-specific guide. Complete its exercise before exploring automation.

## Try it yourself

Pick one learning outcome for this week and bookmark two relevant lessons.

## Common questions

### Is there a required order?

No. The beginner route is a suggestion. You can choose lessons based on your interests and current knowledge.

### Will completing a path give me a certificate?

This learning center does not issue a certificate. It provides free lessons, exercises, and knowledge checks.
