A quick guide to the quizzes

Learn how question cards, navigation, skipped questions, early submission, and retakes work.

THE USEFUL BIT

Choose a quiz and start with the first question card. Select one answer, then use Next to continue.

01

Answer one card at a time

Choose a quiz and start with the first question card. Select one answer, then use Next to continue. Previous lets you revisit earlier cards and change a selection. The progress display shows how many questions you have answered, so you can distinguish navigation from completion.

  • There is no timer or account requirement.
  • You can leave a question unanswered and return to it.
02

Submit when you are ready

You can submit after answering at least one question. If questions remain unanswered, a dialog explains that the results may not be fully accurate. Choose Keep answering to continue or Submit anyway to see a partial result. Submitting a partial quiz is optional.

  • An unanswered question is not marked as a wrong answer.
  • Partial accuracy is calculated only from answered questions.
03

Review and try again

The result shows completion, accuracy on answered questions, and explanations for each item. Use the suggested lesson to review a topic. Retake starts a fresh attempt. Answers are held in the current page session and are not sent to an AI service or saved to an account.

  • Reloading the page resets the current attempt.
  • Keep results private if you use a shared device.
IN PRACTICE

A partial attempt

If you answer four of six questions and get three right, the result shows 4 of 6 answered and 75% accuracy on answered questions. It clearly labels the attempt incomplete.

YOUR TURN

Try one small thing

Open a quiz, answer one question, and inspect the early-submission warning before deciding whether to continue.

A few common questions

Can I submit before finishing?+

Yes, after at least one answer. You will see a warning before an incomplete attempt is submitted.

Are my answers saved?+

No account or server-side answer history is created. The current attempt resets when you reload or leave the page.
