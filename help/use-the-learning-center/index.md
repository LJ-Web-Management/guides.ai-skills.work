# Find your way around

A quick guide to searching lessons, choosing a topic, and finding a useful starting point.

Source: https://lj-web-management.github.io/guides.ai-skills.work/help/use-the-learning-center/
Updated: 2026-10-28
Publisher: ai-skills.work

## Choose a topic that fits your goal

Use Learn AI for the foundations, AI at work for practical workflows, Careers for learning and job questions, and AI for your role for profession-specific examples. You can read in any order. If you are new to the topic, begin with AI, without the overwhelm and Your first steps into AI.

- Every guide is available without an account.
- Each topic page groups related articles in one place.

## Search in your own words

Use the search page to look for a topic, task, or question. Search checks guide titles, descriptions, and lesson text in your browser. Try a shorter phrase if your first search is too specific. Topic filters help narrow the results without hiding the rest of the library permanently.

- Try prompts, privacy, job security, or spreadsheets.
- Clear the search field to return to all guides.

## Use the next useful step

Article contents help you move between sections. Examples show the idea in context, and practice tasks let you try it yourself. Related lessons and quizzes offer a next step. You do not need to finish every article to benefit from one useful improvement to your workflow.

- Use the breadcrumb links to return to a topic.
- Bookmark a page in your browser if you want to revisit it.

## In practice: A good starting route

Read the AI basics lesson, try one prompting exercise, then take the free AI basics quiz. Use the explanations to decide which lesson to revisit.

## Try it yourself

Search for a task you do regularly and find one guide with an exercise you can try using fictional information.

## Common questions

### Do I need an account?

No. The guides and quizzes on this learning center are available without an account. The main ai-skills.work product may have separate requirements.

### Does my search go to an AI service?

No. The learning center searches its static guide index in your browser.
