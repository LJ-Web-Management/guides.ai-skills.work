# Keep your information yours

Understand what to avoid uploading and how to use AI with sensible privacy boundaries.

Source: https://lj-web-management.github.io/guides.ai-skills.work/learn/ai-privacy-and-safety/
Updated: 2026-10-28
Publisher: ai-skills.work

## Recognize sensitive information

Personal details, customer records, internal plans, passwords, and unpublished business information require care. Sensitivity depends on the context, not just the file name. A spreadsheet without names can still identify someone through other details. Before using a tool, check your organization’s policy and any obligations that apply to the information.

- Never paste passwords, access tokens, or private keys.
- Use fictional examples when learning a workflow.

## Understand what the product does with data

Review the current terms, retention settings, sharing controls, and available account protections. Check whether uploads or conversations may be used to improve models and whether your account type changes that treatment. Do not assume every product has the same settings. If something is unclear, ask the responsible person before uploading workplace data.

- Check shared links and workspace membership.
- Use approved accounts and the minimum information required.

## Reduce exposure before you begin

Replace identifying information with synthetic examples whenever possible. Redaction requires more than removing a name: check hidden tabs, document comments, metadata, and combinations of identifying details. If you accidentally share a credential, follow your organization’s incident process and rotate it. Deleting a conversation alone may not resolve the exposure.

- Keep a local original and use a separate sanitized copy.
- Treat retrieved web pages and documents as untrusted input.

## In practice: A safer way to practice

Instead of uploading a real customer list, create five fictional customers with invented order amounts. Test the workflow on that sample, then ask the responsible team which approved system can handle the real records.

## Try it yourself

Choose a document type you use regularly. Write a checklist of information that would need removal or permission before using it with an AI tool.

## Common questions

### Does a paid plan guarantee privacy?

No. Review the specific product, account terms, settings, and your organization’s requirements.

### Are these quizzes sent to an AI model?

No. These quizzes calculate results in your browser. This site does not send your answers to an AI service.
