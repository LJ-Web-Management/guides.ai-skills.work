# AI, without the overwhelm

Understand what artificial intelligence is, how generative AI works, and where human judgment still matters.

## What AI actually means

Artificial intelligence is a broad term for computer systems that perform tasks associated with human abilities, such as recognizing speech, identifying patterns, or generating text. Machine learning is one way to build these systems: the system learns patterns from examples instead of relying only on hand-written rules. A useful starting question is what a particular system does, rather than whether it is intelligent.

- Predictive AI estimates an outcome, such as likely demand.
- Generative AI produces new text, images, audio, or code from learned patterns.

## How a chatbot produces an answer

A language model uses the conversation and patterns learned during training to generate a likely continuation. Depending on the product, it may also search the web, retrieve documents, or use tools. A fluent answer does not establish that any of those checks happened. Ask what evidence supports an answer and inspect the original source when accuracy matters.

- Training information can be incomplete or outdated.
- The same question can produce different answers.

## Where people still matter

AI can help you draft, organize, compare, and explore. It cannot take responsibility for your decisions. You provide the goal, context, and standard of quality. You also decide whether an output fits the real situation. For consequential work, a person with the relevant expertise should check the result before anyone acts on it.

- Check facts against original sources.
- Treat confidence in the wording separately from confidence in the evidence.

## In practice: A useful first task

Give an assistant a fictional meeting note and ask it to extract decisions and next steps. Compare each item with the note. You are testing a specific ability with information you can verify.

## Try it yourself

Write down three tasks you do regularly. For each, decide whether AI could draft, organize, explain, or check something. Pick one low-stakes task to try.

## Common questions

### Is AI the same as a chatbot?

No. Chatbots are one interface for some AI systems. Recommendation systems, image recognition, and forecasting tools are also examples of AI.

### Can AI understand everything it says?

Useful language output does not establish human-like understanding. Evaluate whether a system performs your task reliably.
