THE AI SKILLS FIELD GUIDE

Useful knowledge. Honest guidance.

Our approach to practical AI education, evidence, and responsible learning.

Learning that meets you where you are

The AI Skills Field Guide is a public learning center from ai-skills.work. It brings together beginner explanations, practical exercises, career questions, and informal knowledge quizzes. The aim is to help you make a more informed next step.

How the guides are structured

Each guide includes a direct explanation, an example, a small exercise, common questions, and relevant sources. We favor transferable methods over lists of fashionable tools. We avoid presenting uncertain forecasts as personal predictions.

Evidence and changing information

AI products and workplace practices change. Check linked original sources for current requirements. Publication and update dates identify the content version; they are not a guarantee that every external source has stayed the same. Sources support further investigation and do not imply endorsement by the named organizations.

What the quizzes measure

Quizzes sample selected concepts and provide explanations. They are not credentials, professional assessments, or predictions of employment outcomes. Partial attempts show completion separately from accuracy.

Using the advice

Adapt examples to your situation, follow your organization’s rules, and obtain qualified review for consequential decisions. General educational material does not replace professional advice.

Something needs correcting?

Use the current support options on ai-skills.work. Include the guide URL, the claim in question, and a reliable source where possible. Do not include confidential information.
