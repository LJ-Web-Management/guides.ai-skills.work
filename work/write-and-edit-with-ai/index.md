# Write faster. Still sound like you.

Draft and edit useful writing with AI while preserving accuracy, intention, and your own voice.

## Start with purpose and real material

Give the assistant your audience, purpose, key facts, and desired action. Supply your own notes instead of asking it to invent expertise or experience. Decide whether you need an outline, draft, or critique. These are different tasks, and separating them makes revision easier.

- Identify the one thing the reader should understand or do.
- Mark facts and quotations that must stay unchanged.

## Edit for clarity and voice

Ask for specific changes: remove repetition, shorten sentences, explain jargon, or reorganize an argument. Provide a short sample of your own writing when useful. Review whether the result still means what you intended. Polished language can accidentally remove a caveat, change a commitment, or make an unsupported claim sound certain.

- Compare the revision against the original.
- Keep words you would naturally use.

## Review before publishing

Check names, dates, sources, promises, and originality. Confirm you have permission to use supplied material. Follow your organization’s disclosure policy and any applicable publishing requirements. Read the final piece aloud or ask a colleague to review it when the message is sensitive.

- Remove invented testimonials or credentials.
- Check that the requested action is clear.

## In practice: An editing prompt

Edit this announcement for a general audience. Keep the launch date and eligibility rules unchanged. Cut repetition and explain technical terms. List any ambiguity separately instead of guessing what we mean.

## Try it yourself

Take a paragraph you wrote. Ask for a clearer version, then identify one useful change and one change you would reject.

## Common questions

### Will AI writing always sound generic?

No, but a generic brief often produces generic writing. Specific source material, audience context, and human editing improve the result.

### Can I publish the first draft?

Review it first. You remain responsible for accuracy, permissions, and the commitments it makes.
