# Make your next application more thoughtful

Use AI to research roles, improve a truthful CV, and practice relevant interview answers.

Source: https://lj-web-management.github.io/guides.ai-skills.work/work/ai-for-job-search/
Updated: 2026-10-28
Publisher: ai-skills.work

## Understand the role before applying

Read the job description and identify responsibilities, essential requirements, and evidence the employer is likely to value. Ask AI to organize those requirements, then check the original posting. Research the employer through official sources and avoid relying on invented company facts.

- Distinguish required skills from desirable ones.
- Keep a record of the specific role and application deadline.

## Translate your real experience clearly

Provide factual notes about your experience and ask for a clearer structure. Do not invent achievements, qualifications, or numerical results. Tailor emphasis to the role while keeping the underlying facts consistent. Remove personal details that are unnecessary for drafting and review every claim before submission.

- Use examples of actions and outcomes you can explain.
- Keep formatting readable and follow application instructions.

## Practice answering and asking questions

Ask for one interview question at a time and answer from your own experience. Use feedback to improve clarity and relevance. Verify technical feedback with a trusted source. Prepare thoughtful questions about the team, expectations, and learning support. Follow the employer’s rules on AI use in assessments.

- Practice without reading a generated script.
- Keep a human version of your story in your own words.

## In practice: A truthful CV prompt

Using only these experience notes, suggest three concise bullet points relevant to the support role below. Do not add metrics or qualifications. Ask me for missing evidence instead of inventing it.

## Try it yourself

Choose one job requirement and write a short example of when you demonstrated it. Ask AI to improve the clarity while preserving every fact.

## Common questions

### Can AI guarantee my CV passes an applicant tracking system?

No. Follow the employer’s instructions, use clear relevant language, and avoid claims of guaranteed screening success.

### Should I use AI during an interview?

Follow the employer’s stated rules. Preparing with AI is different from receiving undisclosed assistance during an assessment.
