# AI for finance and accounting

Use AI to support explanations, spreadsheet work, and document organization while retaining professional review.

## Use AI for support, not unchecked authority

An assistant can explain a formula, organize a document, or suggest a reconciliation checklist. Financial decisions and records need reliable evidence and appropriate professional review. Use the current standards and rules applicable to your jurisdiction and organization rather than relying on a generated explanation.

- Keep original records and a clear audit trail.
- Use synthetic data when testing a new workflow.

## Verify calculations and definitions

Calculate important figures in a suitable spreadsheet or accounting system. Check units, currencies, dates, tax treatment, and the meaning of each category. Test formulas against known examples and edge cases. A numerically correct total can still be misleading if it uses the wrong definition or period.

- Reconcile outputs to source records.
- Document assumptions and exceptions.

## Protect records and preserve accountability

Financial information may include personal, commercial, and regulated data. Use approved systems and access controls. AI-generated explanations should not be treated as personalized tax, investment, or legal advice. Assign a responsible reviewer before any output affects reporting or a consequential decision.

- Check retention and sharing requirements.
- Never paste credentials or unrestricted financial records into an unapproved tool.

## In practice: A sensible first task

Use fictional transactions to ask for a spreadsheet formula and explanation. Test it against a hand-calculated result, a refund, and a missing value before considering an approved workplace application.

## Try it yourself

Create a five-item review checklist for an AI-assisted spreadsheet containing totals, currency, dates, exclusions, and source reconciliation.

## Common questions

### Can AI file or approve financial records for me?

Capabilities vary, but consequential submissions need authorized workflows, controls, and professional review.

### Is a financial explanation from AI advice?

A general explanation is not a substitute for advice from an appropriately qualified professional who understands your circumstances.
