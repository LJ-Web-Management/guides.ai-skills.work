# AI for teaching and learning

Prepare learning activities, adapt explanations, and support practice with careful review and clear expectations.

Updated: 2026-10-28

## Begin with a learning outcome

Decide what learners should be able to explain or do. Ask AI to suggest activities and examples that support that outcome. Check factual accuracy, age appropriateness, accessibility, and alignment with your curriculum. A polished worksheet can still assess the wrong skill.

- Specify the learner’s level and available resources.
- Review examples for cultural assumptions and bias.

## Support practice and useful feedback

Create varied questions, worked examples, and hints. Verify answer keys independently, including alternative valid answers. Ask learners to explain their reasoning and apply ideas to new examples. Use AI assistance to make practice more accessible while preserving meaningful opportunities to think.

- Match feedback to the specific error.
- Avoid treating fluent writing as proof of understanding.

## Set expectations and protect learners

Follow institutional rules for AI use, safeguarding, consent, and student information. Explain permitted assistance and acknowledgement requirements. Do not upload identifiable student work to an unapproved service. Teachers remain responsible for judging suitability and making consequential assessments.

- Provide an alternative when access to a tool is unequal.
- Review automated feedback before relying on it.

## In practice: A useful classroom exercise

Present a short generated explanation with one deliberate error. Ask learners to identify it using a trusted reference and explain the correction. The activity practices evidence checking as well as subject knowledge.

## Try it yourself

Choose a learning outcome and draft one explanation, one practice question, and one way to check independent understanding.

## Common questions

### Can AI mark every assignment?

Automated marking can miss context and valid alternatives. Use institution-approved processes and appropriate human oversight for consequential assessment.

### Should students disclose AI use?

Follow the institution’s policy and explain the expectations clearly before an assignment begins.
