AI for HR and recruitment

Improve communications and learning materials while protecting people’s information and reviewing fairness.

THE USEFUL BIT

01

Improve communication with clear criteria

Use AI to clarify a job description, onboarding checklist, or training outline from approved material. Check whether requirements are relevant to the role and language is accessible. Generated text can reproduce assumptions or bias, so review the substance as well as the tone.

  • Separate essential requirements from preferences.
  • Verify policies and benefits against authoritative internal sources.
02

Treat candidate and employee data carefully

Applications, performance information, and employee questions can contain sensitive personal details. Use only approved systems and the minimum necessary information. Understand applicable employment, privacy, and discrimination rules before considering an automated assessment process.

  • Use synthetic examples during training.
  • Check access, retention, and vendor arrangements.
03

Keep consequential decisions accountable

Do not assume a generated ranking is fair or valid because it is numerical. Any system affecting hiring, promotion, or employment needs appropriate legal, fairness, and operational review. Provide human oversight and a route to challenge or correct inaccurate information.

  • Document the criteria and evaluation method.
  • Test for errors and unequal effects before adoption.
IN PRACTICE

A low-risk starting point

Ask an approved assistant to simplify a public job description while preserving essential requirements. A recruiter and hiring manager check relevance, accessibility, and policy compliance before publication.

YOUR TURN

Try one small thing

Review a fictional job description. Highlight vague requirements and rewrite them into observable, role-relevant criteria.

A few common questions

Can AI choose the best candidate?+

A model’s output does not establish job suitability or fairness. Consequential selection requires valid criteria, appropriate oversight, and compliance with applicable rules.

What is a good training use?+

Drafting a general onboarding outline from approved policies is a useful starting task when a responsible person reviews it.
